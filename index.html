How it works

From uncertainty to a clearer next step.

Students often know what they are studying, but not necessarily what employers will expect, which paths fit them, or what they should build before graduation.

Career Trajectory is designed around a simple sequence: understand your current position, explore realistic directions, identify what is missing, then decide what to do next.

The Trajectory Model

Your current position is the starting point, not the verdict.
