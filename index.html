MEET THE FOUNDER

Saman Kayani

Founder, Career Trajectory

Career Trajectory was founded by Saman Kayani, a lecturer and researcher in Management Sciences with a focus on Human Resource Management, digital transformation and the changing world of work.

Through teaching and working with students, she saw a recurring problem: students are often told to “build skills” and “prepare for the future,” but they are rarely shown which skills they actually need for the career they want.

Career Trajectory was created to bridge that gap by helping students understand their career options, identify their skill gaps and turn that insight into practical next steps.

How it works

From uncertainty to a clearer next step.

Students often know what they are studying, but not necessarily what employers will expect, which paths fit them, or what they should build before graduation.

Career Trajectory is designed around a simple sequence: understand your current position, explore realistic directions, identify what is missing, then decide what to do next.
